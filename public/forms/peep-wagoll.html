PEEP: Personal Emergency Evacuation Plan

Written as a care plan row
What do I need help with?

I am aware that my mobility is poor and therefore I will require support to evacuate in case of emergency.

What can help me?

Alerting the patient

Ward staff will calmly inform you of the emergency and the need to evacuate the building.

Clear instructions

Staff will provide you with simple, clear instructions for what they need you to do. Staff will ensure you have access to your mobility aids such as walking stick or wheelchair if quick evacuation is necessary.

Safe transfer techniques

As you can walk, staff will provide physical support such as providing you with your mobility aids and will stay with you to lead you to the exit. During evacuation, staff will provide encouragement and reassurance, as well as ensuring that there are no obstacles for you to get out as quickly and safely as possible. If necessary, staff will ask you to use a wheelchair in order to ensure a fast evacuation.

Actions needed
Staff perspectiveStaff will alert you to the emergency and support you to evacuate.
Patient perspectiveListening to the instructions of staff in order to evacuate in a safe and timely manner.
Why it reads well: it is written to the patient, in their care plan, and it describes what will physically happen rather than stating a principle. Three questions answered - how am I told, what am I asked to do, how am I helped out.
