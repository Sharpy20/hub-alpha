## What this is

A list of the guides currently built, grouped by the part of the Trust best placed to
check them. The grouping is a suggestion, not a proposal to defend - move anything that
sits in the wrong place.

**This is not a request to review 68 documents.** It is a request to agree who owns each
group. Content would go to an owner only when it is ready, and only for the guides the
pilot wards actually ask for.

Each guide is assembled from Trust policy and national guidance and arrives largely
complete, with any contradictions between its sources flagged. What it needs is a quick
expert eyeball from someone with knowledge and seniority in that area - a reading job
rather than a writing job. A guide does not appear on the site without its owner's
sign-off, and that gate is already built.

The list will grow. Older adult content is being added ahead of the trial, and adoption
naturally grows the number, which is exactly why sign-off is better shared out than held
by one person.

## Two suggestions

**Let the pilot wards choose.** Rather than working through all 68, the two trial wards
could say which guides they actually want. That gives a realistic first tranche, gives the
wards ownership of the content they use, and tests the guide-building process against real
demand.

**A couple of groups are natural starting points.** The Safeguarding set is the largest
group built entirely from the Trust's own training material. The HR and payroll set carries
no clinical risk at all, so it could be used to test the sign-off route end to end before
any clinical time is spent on it.
