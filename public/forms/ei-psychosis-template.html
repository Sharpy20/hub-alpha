Referral criteria
  • Aged 14-64
  • First episode of psychosis; prior treatment not exceeding 12 months in secondary care or 3 years post initial treatment
  • Co-morbid substance misuse, personality disorders, or forensic / offending history are not grounds for exclusion.
  • Drug Induced Psychosis is not excluded, but ongoing psychosis must be differentiated from intoxication.
1. Symptoms suggestive of a First Episode Psychosis
Thought disorder
i.e. jumbled speech, rapid topic changes, making up words, strange ideas, difficulty concentrating or misunderstanding questions, thought block etc.		 If yes, what do they present with / how do they present?
Delusional beliefs
i.e. deeply held convictions that can significantly impact a person's behaviour and perception of reality. Transient nature / insight.		 If yes, please give a brief description as to what, including frequency / intensity / distress.
Paranoia
i.e. feeling of intense distrust, suspicion or fear of harm to themselves / others.		 If yes, please give a brief description as to what, including frequency / intensity / distress.
Hallucinations
i.e. auditory, visual, tactile, olfactory, gustatory experiences excluding those attributed to dissociative features related to trauma, Autistic overwhelm and sensory overload.		 If yes, please give a brief description as to what, including frequency / intensity / distress.
Questions 2 to 10
2. Do they have insight into their experiences?
3. What is the duration of their symptoms?
4. Is there an impact on social, occupational and personal functioning?
5. Please provide a brief summary of risk history and current
6. Is there any family history of mental illness?
7. Is there any relevant treatment history?
i.e. psychotropics, mood stabilisers, steroids, antidepressants etc
8. Are there any other mental health / neurodevelopmental diagnoses?
i.e. Anxiety, Depression, Personality Disorder's, Bipolar Disorder, Autism, Learning Disability, ADHD, PTSD etc.
9. Has there been any drug and alcohol misuse?
10. Is there any other relevant background information?
i.e. Trauma, Organic, Genetic or Acquired brain injury etc.
Check eligibility before you write it. EI is for first-episode psychosis only. If the person has already been discharged from EI it is unlikely they will be accepted again - refer to CMHT instead.
