Informal Inpatient Admission

Discuss each point with the patient and tick the box once they have understood and agreed it.

1. Date of Admission:  
2Your Rights as an Informal Patient
I understand I am free to leave the hospital at any time unless assessed otherwise under the Mental Health Act.
I will be involved in decisions about my care and treatment.
My confidentiality and privacy will be maintained, subject to safety requirements.
3Leaving the Ward
I will discuss any leave from the ward with staff in advance.
I will inform staff before leaving and return by the agreed time.
I understand leave may be restricted for my safety.
4Safety and Security Rules
I will not bring alcohol or illicit substances onto the ward.
I will not engage in violence, threats, or aggression.
I will inform staff immediately if I feel unsafe.
I will not bring restricted items (e.g., lighters, sharp objects) onto the ward.
5Smoking
I understand that I cannot smoke whilst on the wards or on the grounds of the hospital.
I understand that I cannot keep lighters, matches, cigarettes or any tobacco on me in my belongings. Anything brought on to the ward will be disposed of.
I understand that I can only vape the Trust purchased vapes (this is due to safety and to ensure that the vapes used will not set off fire alarms).
6Searching
I understand that safety checks and searches will be carried out. (This is to ensure your safety and the safety of others.)
I understand that ALL parcels and deliveries, including food deliveries will be searched. (If you do not wish for items to be searched please make alternative arrangements to have deliveries sent to family or friends).
7Respect and Behaviour
I will treat everyone with kindness and respect.
I will not use discriminatory, racial or abusive language and this will be reported as a criminal act.
I will respect other patients' privacy and space.
I will keep noise to a minimum, especially during quiet hours.
8Treatment and Care Planning
I will participate in care planning and treatment decisions.
I will discuss any concerns about medication or therapy with staff.
I will attend regular reviews with my care team.
9Mobile Phones and Devices
I will follow the ward policy regarding device use.
I will not record or photograph other patients or staff.
I understand that I may not have my own room as some wards are dormitories with shared bathrooms and toilets. I am agreeing to an informal admission and I will adhere to the Trust policies and procedures.
I confirm that I have discussed the above points with the patient and they understand their rights and responsibilities as an informal inpatient.
