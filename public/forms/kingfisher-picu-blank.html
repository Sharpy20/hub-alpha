Once received, this referral will be reviewed by the relevant members of the Multi-Disciplinary Team.

You or your service line will be made aware of the outcome of the referral as soon as possible, this will be once triaging of the referral has been completed. An entry will also be made on the individuals Electronic Patient Record.

PYSCHIATRIC INTENSIVE CARE UNIT - INCLUSION CRITERIA

Please see NAPICU guidance for full explanation of inclusion criteria.

Key indicators for a referral to Kingfisher House:

  • Externally directed aggression. A patient is assessed as posing a significant risk of harm to others, or extreme aggression towards property as a result of their current mental health presentation.
  • Internally directed aggression. A patient is assessed as posing a significant risk of suicide and the patient is unresponsive to preventative measures available.
  • Absconding. Patients who are detained under the MHA 1983, for whom the consequences of persistent absconding are serious enough to warrant treatment in the PICU. The PICU should not provide security for its own sake, there should always be a primary clinical reason for admission to prevent absconding.
  • Unpredictably. Unpredictable patients, potentially posing a significant risk to self or others and requiring further assessment.
PYSCHIATRIC INTENSIVE CARE UNIT - EXCLUSION CRITERIA

Please see NAPICU guidance for full explanation of exclusion criteria.

Key indicators:

  • The presenting risk is too high a risk for a PICU admission and would require a medium or high secure PICU.
  • The patient has a primary diagnosis of substance misuse, and the primary purpose of admission is solely to prevent access to substances.
  • The patient's physical condition is too frail to allow their safe management in a PICU.
  • The patient is restricted (subject to restrictions under the MHA 1983, via the courts, prisons or Ministry of Justice) and has no clear pathway or provision for transfer from the PICU once clinically warranted.
  • The patient has the following a primary diagnosis:
    • Dementia.
    • Learning Disability (LD) and requires a specialist LD facility.
