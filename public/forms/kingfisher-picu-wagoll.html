REASON FOR REFERRAL

Please provide as much detail as possible to support rationale for referral. Please include details regarding mental state, engagement and compliance with treatment.

John Doe is posing a significant risk to others which is difficult to manage safely on an open acute ward. John is on a Section 2 for assessment, his mental health has deteriorated, and he has been displaying risky behaviour prior to admission, which family report is out of character.

Whilst on the ward, John's risks have continued to escalate, and he has proven a risk to self and others. John needs a full assessment of his mental health following the deterioration, which needs to be done in a more secure environment where risks to others will be minimised.

History: John is an 18-year-old gentleman who has not had any previous involvement with mental health services until recently. He was known to social services and under child protection as his mother was a victim of domestic abuse from his father; it is reported that John used to hold knives when his father came round due to the risk he posed. In March this year, John stabbed someone with a bladed article causing GBH. John left school and went to college however due to poor attendance did not complete, but enjoyed fixing cars and electric scooters.

John's mental state deteriorated around his 18th birthday. Family were attempting to support John at home with crisis team support however, following a ligature where John was found with a rope around his neck, the family took him to A&E. Family report that risk behaviours had increased, he had been taking paracetamol overdoses and swallowing parts of his mobile phone, reporting hearing voices and expressing paranoid, suspicious beliefs about people trying to kill him and his family.

John was admitted to the ward on [DATE], nursed on level 3 observations.

Since admission

  • 23/12 - John has taken a pen and threatened to stab himself; when staff intervened, he threatened to stab a staff member.
  • 24/12 - John was asked to provide a UDS. He punched and broke the paper towel dispenser in the dorm, SAS alarms activated, John wrapped himself in the curtain and punched the radiator, refused PRN offered. John continued presenting as chaotic in manner and still a risk to self. He then became hostile to staff, entering personal space, blowing into staff's face and threatening to assault - SAS alarms pulled and John appeared to de-escalate. Went to de-escalation with staff at his request, grabbed keys from staff; staff intervened to remove these which caused John to become so resistive and aggressive that seclusion was required. John remained in seclusion for 1 hour as his level of risk to others quickly reduced, however whilst in seclusion John removed a bolt from the light on the ceiling, covered up the observation window with the sheet and stood up his mattress and placed himself behind it, obscuring staff's view.
  • 25/12 - John made himself a pot noodle from the kitchen and as a staff member walked past, he threw it at his face and neck. John reported that the staff member had raped his mum and that was the reason for the assault.
  • 26/12 - John was in the de-escalation area and set off the smoke alarms whilst vaping.
  • 26/12 - John secreted a fork from the dining room and refused to hand this back to staff initially. Unclear of his intentions, however as he has stabbed someone previously, staff felt the risk posed to others was high at this moment.
  • 27/12 - John entered the kitchen with staff, which was locked due to his risks, and appeared to take something from the sink. Staff attempted to retrieve this, John would not engage and therefore staff used P&S techniques to search; this resulted in John requiring seclusion as he lunged towards staff and attempted to attack them.

Currently nursed on level 2 observations due to the risk he poses to others.

CURRENT RISKS

Please identify all current risks, including to self, others and property.

To others: GBH - assaulted someone with a bladed article. Threatened to stab staff. Threatened to assault staff. Threw boiling hot pot noodle at staff's face. Secreted a fork and refused to hand it back. Secreted an item from the kitchen. Secluded after attempting to attack staff following a search.

To self: taken to A&E by family following an incident where John was found purple-faced with a rope around his neck; previous risk behaviours include overdoses and swallowing parts of his mobile phone. Threatened to stab himself with a pen.

Risk behaviours appeared to start recently - no previous involvement with MH services prior to this admission.

  • PRN lorazepam prescribed

EXPECTATION OF PICU ADMISSION

To complete an assessment of John's mental health to establish symptoms, diagnosis and treatment plan while maintaining the safety of others. John is reporting some delusional ideas about staff. Prior to admission he reported hearing voices for the past 2 to 3 weeks, however the nursing team have not observed any evidence of responding to unseen stimuli. Due to minimal information on SystmOne there are also unknown risks associated.
