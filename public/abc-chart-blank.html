ABC Chart (behavioural)

Record the facts of what happened. Describe behaviour objectively - do not interpret.

Behaviour being considered
1. Date & time
2. Where did it happen?
3. Person(s) / carer(s) present
4. Antecedent - what was the person doing just before?
5. Behaviour - the facts of what happened. Do NOT interpret.
6. Consequence - what happened afterwards / how did it end?
7. How did the person appear BEFORE the behaviour?
8. Other (before)
9. How did the person appear DURING the behaviour?
10. Other (during)
