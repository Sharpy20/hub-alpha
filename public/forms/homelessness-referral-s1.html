Homelessness Referral

SystmOne questionnaire · two sections
Patient Details
1. Date of referral
2. Reason for referral
Please provide details such as dates of being homeless, where the patient has been staying prior to admission, and what steps they have taken to prevent homelessness i.e. contacted council services, engaging with social worker etc.
3. Last known address
4. NHS number
5. Current ward
6. What benefits does the patient receive and total amount of payments received per month?
Patient Circumstance
1. Is the patient homeless?Yes  /  No
2. Do they have any criminal convictions, eg: Arson, GBH, Sex offender etc?
3. Is the patient fleeing domestic abuse?
4. If any, what current community support does the patient have in place upon discharge, eg Social worker, CPN, Enablement? If applicable please provide contact details below
5. Current diagnosis and medication, can the patient administer their own medication?
Why the detail matters. The convictions and domestic abuse answers decide what accommodation the council can safely offer. The benefits figure decides what the person can afford. Depot arrangements and whether they self-administer decide whether a placement can support the medication. A blank here means a slower placement, not a kinder one.
Consent: the Duty to Refer is built around the patient's agreement. Give them the housing contact details, offer to help them self-refer, and record that the offer was made.
